# The Unforgiven — Campaign Handoff

## Source note

This document combines:

- the structured Elligaesia world export
- the original `Unforgiven(1).docx` campaign document
- development conversations about the campaign

Some naming/details differ between sources. Those discrepancies are explicitly noted rather than silently corrected.

## Campaign overview

Structured campaign data describes **The Unforgiven** as a completed campaign centered on strange disappearances in Reqrun, the awakening of an ancient Aboleth, and discovery of the sorcerer's identity.

The original campaign document begins with animals disappearing from the small town of Reqrun at night. The situation escalates when the governor's son, Jackson, disappears. The governor hires the party, known locally as the **3 Misfits**, to investigate.

The party includes:

- Harry, a halfling
- a Wood Elf from the Northern Woods of Weshire
- Rowan, a human with her familiar Moose

Structured data identifies Harry, Rowan, and Aerith as player characters. The original adventure document uses a Wood Elf member and the later structured export identifies that character as Aerith.

## Reqrun

Reqrun is a town in Batik. It became the center of the Aboleth Crisis and later the White Rabbit Incident.

The town is historically important because it is a stopping point for trade into western cities and travelers passing toward the mountain pass.

The original campaign notes say:

- animals have been disappearing for months
- disappearances happen at night
- Jackson, the governor's son, disappears
- townspeople initially suspect various farmers or the wizard
- the town has a history involving a major wizard/necromancer conflict at the mountain lake
- the town was named after the wizard according to the adventure notes
- the old wizard reportedly removed trolls from the woods and made passage safer
- an Abeloth was seen during the wizard's era

## Important Reqrun NPCs

### Pattrice

Governor of Reqrun. The structured data describes him as well respected and having led the town into a small golden age where trade is good, no one goes hungry, and everyone has a place to live.

The capital building is being rebuilt after a fire/storm-related incident. The original adventure notes intentionally make Pattrice's responses potentially suspicious.

### Jackson

Pattrice's son and the missing child whose disappearance escalates the crisis.

### Nedda

Halfling innkeeper of The Mandolin. She inherited the inn from her father and runs it strictly. She employs two dwarf barbarians.

The structured data says she is distrustful of Orcs, Elves, and Wizards. Her family's inn has existed for generations. Her father died during an Orc raid many years ago.

One of Nedda's dwarf employees was influenced by the sorcerer and attacked the party.

### Frank

Reqrun blacksmith. Reserved, honest, monotone, respected by townspeople, and normally makes equipment for farmers and supplies for the town barracks.

Frank fishes at the northern lake. He hears strange noises there.

During the disappearances he is secretly under the sorcerer's magical influence and is forced to assist against his will.

A farmer sees him sneaking around on the evening Jackson disappears. Frank claims he was fishing at the northern lake.

People under the sorcerer's control show a **faint green glow in their eyes**.

### Fredrick

Human farmer south/east of the river. Friendly, cautious, shy. He raises pigs and grows grain.

He initially reports only one missing pig, but the party can discover many more animals, including dogs, have disappeared.

Structured campaign data says Fredrick was under the Aboleth's influence.

### Orivom

Friendly dwarf farmer south of the river. Likes fishing, good company, and cooking. Somewhat superstitious and knowledgeable about dangers around the area. Warns travelers about the mountain lake north of Reqrun.

### Shana

Librarian. Knows about multiple disappearances, especially pets. Can provide town history if questioned.

The town's history includes a great battle between a wizard and necromancer at the mountain lake.

### Emmyth Carfir

Elf teacher/wizard associated with the School of Reqrun and healing magic.

He knows the commonly accepted history of the town may be wrong. He can direct the party to the library for more information.

### Faela Logleragle

A woman placed under the sorcerer's magical influence. The structured data explicitly says controlled people displayed a faint green glow in their eyes.

Faela attacked Rowan while controlled. Rowan killed Faela while defending herself.

### Narle

Witnessed Rowan escaping with Moose after the confrontation with Faela. Rowan convinced Narle that she was not responsible for Faela's death.

## Player character canon relevant to The Unforgiven

### Rowan

Human swordswoman/mercenary.

She was raised in Baldon by her blacksmith father, who taught her to fight with a sword. She discovered Moose trapped in a cage and freed him, forming a bond that allowed her to communicate with animals.

Her family was ambushed by Orcs while returning from a supply trip. Her father was killed by a stab in the back. Rowan killed the remaining Orcs, swore revenge, and later joined a mercenary group where she met Aerith.

During The Unforgiven, Faela attacked Rowan while under sorcerer control. Rowan killed Faela while defending herself and escaped with Moose. Narle witnessed the escape and believed Rowan was not responsible for Faela's death.

### Harry

Halfling member of the 3 Misfits. Structured data says his ring became important in encounters with the sorcerer and influenced creatures.

### Aerith

Structured world data identifies Aerith as a player character and as a High Elf connected to the Stratos storyline. The original Unforgiven document describes the third party member as a Wood Elf from the Northern Woods of Weshire; verify current PC data before treating every detail as finalized.

## Core mystery / actual threat

The original adventure notes identify the immediate cause of the animal disappearances as an **Abeloth/Aboleth** whose home was disturbed by construction of the new capitol building.

The creature lives in the lake north of the larger lake and has been taking animals to regain strength.

Its influence is spreading:

- taking control of more creatures
- slowly taking over the town with its mind
- poisoning the river with slime
- causing animals to become sick
- preparing to use animals to attack the town by the third night

## The lake

The northern lake contains dead fish and has a strong smell of decay.

The lake receives a waterfall from the mountains and has two outflowing rivers to the south; one is believed to feed the stream through town.

The party can detect a faint foreign substance in the water.

The adventure notes explicitly connect the pollution to the creature's slime.

## Forest / northern route

The adventure contains a northern journey with multiple possible encounters.

Notable elements include:

- a hidden/overgrown path
- forest creatures
- a Shambling Mound containing the missing scout
- a group connected to a mysterious forest balance
- a Green Hag encounter
- three Will-o'-Wisps

The Green Hag tests whether the party is worthy. When defeated, she reveals a path back to the hidden path to the lake.

## Green Hag encounter

The Green Hag and Will-o'-Wisps guard/complicate access to the lake.

At defeat she says, in substance, that the party may be worthy and reveals a path to the lake.

The forest appears to brighten after her defeat.

## Dronath / the Abeloth

The campaign document gives the Abeloth a name: **Dronath**.

Dronath explains that his mother lived in the cave under the great lake and ruled the lake and city above it.

A sorcerer from the northern mountains tried to control Dronath's mother. A wizard sought to destroy her.

In the ancient battle:

- the wizard fell
- Dronath's mother devoured the wizard
- the sorcerer was badly wounded and escaped
- Dronath's mother remained in the caves to recover
- she entered a deep sleep while reliving the memories of the wizard she had consumed

The adventure describes memory visions from the Abeloth's perspective, including a much larger creature and two beings fighting with magic.

## Sorcerer / necromancer mystery

The old town history says a wizard defeated a necromancer at the mountain lake.

The campaign notes intentionally invert that apparent history:

- the "old wizard" was actually the necromancer
- the necromancer tried to trap/control the Abeloth
- he failed and put it into a deep sleep
- the Abeloth believes the necromancer still exists in town
- the necromancer is actually the **governor, Pattrice**
- the party should initially be encouraged to suspect the wizard

The campaign notes describe the governor as having a strong influence over the town while maintaining a respected public identity.

## Wizard confrontation

The party eventually confronts the healer/wizard in town. He gives up immediately because he is not a fighter.

Depending on the path taken, the wizard may sacrifice himself. The Abeloth can use his magic to restore strength.

If the party defeats the Abeloth, the wizard can reveal that the necromancer never truly left and has a strong influence in the town.

## Cave / hidden lair

The cave contains:

- ancient statues of monsters and deities
- a statue of Bane
- Bane's seal: a closed hand with green light shining from it
- abandoned rooms
- stone chairs/benches
- remains of a library
- a ritual circle
- a deep pool

The party may determine that the location was associated with the sorcerer.

## Bane

The adventure introduces Bane imagery in the ancient cave.

Bane is described as seeking to control everyone and everything, acting slowly and methodically, with cult followers throughout the kingdom.

The structured world bible describes Bane as a powerful sorcerer who was banished after interfering with mortals.

## Campaign outcome in structured data

The structured campaign record says:

- the party uncovered the truth behind the disappearances
- the party confronted the sorcerer and Aboleth
- Reqrun was liberated from the sorcerer's influence
- events surrounding the Aboleth and sorcerer's past revealed deeper forces affecting the world

The campaign is marked `completed` in the structured export.
