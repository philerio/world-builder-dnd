# White Rabbit Incident

## Structured campaign canon

The **White Rabbit Incident** occurred during a festival in Reqrun.

A magical fountain was struck by emerald lightning. The resulting magic empowered a White Rabbit, which created an army of empowered rabbits.

The party ultimately defeated the White Rabbit using the **Holy Hand Grenade**.

Afterward:

- Bill became known as the **Defender of Reqrun**.
- Albert became known as the **Guardian of the Roads**.

## Albert

Albert is a storm mule who was struck by lightning and gained extraordinary powers.

The world bible describes him as associated with green lightning and as wandering the land protecting those in need.

The developer has described Albert as a recurring/future world figure rather than merely a one-off joke NPC. Future Albert sightings are a known plot-hook category.

## Bill

Bill is associated with Reqrun and the White Rabbit Incident.

The structured world export says he recovered his family's greatsword after it had been sold by his son and now protects Reqrun.

He is known as the Defender of Reqrun.

## Holy Hand Grenade

A magic artifact stored in the church at Reqrun.

It was used to defeat the empowered White Rabbit.

## Product/world implication

Reqrun has now survived at least two major supernatural crises:

1. The Unforgiven / Aboleth Crisis
2. The White Rabbit Incident

This history should make Reqrun feel like a persistent location whose NPCs and institutions remember prior campaigns.
